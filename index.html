I'm Aadil, a Computer Science student at NYU Abu Dhabi (Class of 2027), with minors in Applied Mathematics and Economics. I like problems where a working system matters more than a slide deck.

This summer at Inception (G42) I built Model Context Protocol tools and agent workflows that let LLMs work inside Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Planner. Now I'm on UNDP AltFinLab's Digital, AI & Innovation team, building AI tools that non-technical staff can own, and on the robotics track at NYUAD's REACH Lab.

On the research side, I study how much energy software and AI actually use. I co-authored a research agenda on green AI and benchmark Python against C++ translations on LeetCode-hard problems.

I've also spent time on the venture side: scouting startups for Toyota at H-Farm in Venice, interviewing GCC founders at Arrayah in Dubai, and screening student startups for startAD with Violet Ventures. Off the keyboard: chess, football, and emerging-markets investing.
