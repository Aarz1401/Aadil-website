Main information about me

Hi, I'm Aadil Chasmawala

I'm a Computer Science student at New York University Abu Dhabi (Class of 2027), minoring in Applied Mathematics and Economics. I like problems where a working system matters more than a slide deck.

This summer at Inception (G42) I built Model Context Protocol tools and agent workflows that let LLMs work inside Outlook, Teams, SharePoint and Planner. I'm now on UNDP AltFinLab's Digital, AI & Innovation team, and on the robotics track at NYUAD's REACH Lab.

On the research side I study how much energy software and AI actually use, and co-authored a research agenda on green AI. I've also worked on the venture side: scouting startups for Toyota at H-Farm in Venice, and interviewing GCC founders at Arrayah in Dubai.
